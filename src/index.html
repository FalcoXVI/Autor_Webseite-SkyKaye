Welcome to my worlds

I have a habit of creating complicated people and making their lives considerably more complicated.

Sometimes that means surviving a war on an island ruled by vampires. Sometimes it's an academic rival who absolutely refuses to stop being annoyingly perfect. And sometimes it's crossing the seas in pursuit of a very personal vendetta.

Different worlds. Different stories. And plenty more still waiting to be told.

About me

Hey y'all! I'm Sky Kaye, a writer based in Vienna with a habit of creating fictional people, getting far too attached to them, and then making their lives considerably more difficult.

My stories tend to wander between genres. Sometimes that means post-war fantasy and an island full of vampires, sometimes university students turning academic competition into something far more complicated, and sometimes pirates chasing secrets across the sea. What connects them all is what I love writing most: characters. Their relationships, their flaws, the things they carry with them, and the people they find along the way.

The Last Druid, my first completed novel, is currently making its way through the querying trenches while I continue writing its sequel, Rising Tide. At the same time, I'm slowly bringing two very different stories to life with I'd Hate To Love You and Know My Name.

When I'm not writing, I'm usually all up in some music, gaming, spending far too long thinking about stories I haven't written yet, or somehow finding another idea to add to the list.

This website is a home for all of them — the finished stories, the ones still taking shape, and whatever comes next.
