Privacy Policy

How this website handles personal data — and how little of it there is.

General Information

The protection of your personal data is important to me. This website is designed to collect and process as little personal data as possible.

This information may include:

The processing of this information is based on the legitimate interest in providing a secure and functional website pursuant to Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

04

Contact and Contact Form

If you contact me directly by email or through the contact form provided on this website, the information you provide will be processed insofar as necessary to receive, handle and respond to your enquiry.

When you use the contact form, the following information is processed:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Message content
  • Technical information transmitted with the submission, such as your IP address, browser type, the page from which the form was sent and the time of submission

The contact form is provided using Formspree, a service operated by Formspree, Inc. When you submit the form, your browser sends the information you entered directly to Formspree, which forwards it to my email address. Formspree also uses the technical information listed above to keep its service secure and to filter out spam and fraudulent submissions.

Contact form provider

Formspree, Inc.
United States

I have configured Formspree not to store submissions in the submission archive of my Formspree account. Independently of this, Formspree's privacy policy states that it retains information for as long as necessary to provide its services and to comply with its legal obligations, and that it keeps log files for security purposes.

Formspree processes data in the United States and in other countries in which it operates, and relies on service providers that are predominantly based in the United States. Formspree states that it transfers personal data only to countries to which such a transfer is permitted by law and takes steps to ensure that the data continues to be appropriately protected. Please note that data protection laws in these countries, including the rights of authorities to access personal data, may differ from those in the European Union.

Because Formspree does not state an EU-recognised safeguard for this transfer, such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses, the contact form asks for your explicit consent before your message is sent. The transfer of your data to Formspree in the United States is based on this consent pursuant to Article 49(1)(a) GDPR. A note that you have consented, together with the time of consent, is sent along with your message. If you do not wish to consent, you can contact me directly by email instead.

Messages sent through the contact form, as well as emails you send me directly, are received in my Gmail email account. For users in the European Economic Area, Gmail is provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Google may process data on servers outside the European Union, including in the United States. For such transfers, Google relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, under which Google LLC is certified, and on standard contractual clauses. Further information can be found in Google's Privacy Policy.

The processing of information submitted through the contact form is based on the legitimate interest in receiving and responding to enquiries pursuant to Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Where your enquiry relates to steps taken at your request before entering into a contract or to an existing contractual relationship, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR may additionally apply.

Messages I receive by email, whether sent to me directly or forwarded by Formspree, are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the relevant enquiry and any subsequent correspondence, unless longer retention is required for legal reasons.

Further information about the processing of data by Formspree can be found in Formspree's Privacy Policy.
